muxer is a small relay that runs on your Mac. It talks to cmux through its local control socket and listens on 127.0.0.1 only. Your phone reaches it through an HTTPS proxy that only your devices can reach. Tailscale is the easiest way; another VPN, a trusted local network, or a Cloudflare Tunnel behind Cloudflare Access also work.

Set up

You need cmux 0.65 or later on a Mac, and a way for your Android device to reach it over HTTPS.
The steps below use Tailscale, signed in on both.

  1. Install the relay

    brew install gantoine/tap/muxer

  2. Let the relay talk to cmux

    In cmux, open Settings › Automation, set socket access to Password, and set a password.

  3. Start it at login and make it reachable

    muxer install
tailscale serve --bg --https=8765 http://127.0.0.1:8765
muxer doctor

  4. Pair your phone

    Install the APK from the releases page, then scan the QR code this prints:

    muxer pair

Security

  • The relay listens on 127.0.0.1 and forwards only the cmux commands the app uses.
  • Each phone gets its own random token. The computer stores only its SHA-256 hash; the phone keeps it encrypted in the Android Keystore.
  • Pairing codes work once, expire after 10 minutes, and stop after 10 wrong guesses.
  • The app accepts only HTTPS with certificates from a public authority.
  • Revoke a lost phone with muxer revoke.
